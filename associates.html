Beyond the group

Where the group ends, the network begins

Greenwich Meridian Logistics has a vast network of partners and agents across the world. Outside the six group offices, cargo is handled by vetted network partners working to the standards of the alliances we belong to.

That membership is not decoration. It is what puts a documented financial protection framework and an agreed service standard behind a handover in a country where we do not have our own office.

  • FIATA International and FFFAI for freight forwarding practice
  • WCA Inter Global and U-Connect Worldwide for agent coverage
  • NORTHSTAR GLOBAL for FCL forwarding
  • OPCA for project and heavy-lift movements
  • TCDA for tank container work
All memberships and certifications
