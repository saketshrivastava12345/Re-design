Nothing on this website is an offer capable of acceptance. Rates, schedules, transit times and service availability are confirmed only in a written quotation or booking confirmation issued by GML for a specific shipment.

3. The instruments

The calculators and reference tables on this site — the dimension calculator, the currency converter, the container specifications, the world ports directory and the world time clocks — are provided as working aids.

  • The dimension calculator applies the standard divisors (1 CBM = 1,000 kg for sea; the IATA divisor of 6,000 for air). Carriers round and may apply different bases by contract. Its output is an estimate, not a quotation.
  • The currency converter uses a third-party public reference feed, or a rate you enter yourself. Reference rates are indicative mid-market rates. They are not the customs notified rate, not a bank rate, and not a rate at which any transaction will be executed.
  • Container specifications are nominal figures for standard ISO types. Actual tare and payload are those marked on the individual unit's data plate.
  • UN/LOCODE and port data are for reference. Codes change; confirm against the UN/LOCODE register or your carrier's booking note before using one on a document.
  • Clocks are rendered from your own device's clock and time zone database.

Do not rely on any of these outputs for a customs declaration, a contractual commitment or a financial decision without independent verification.

Those conditions, together with the terms recorded on the transport document issued for the shipment, govern the contract of carriage — including the basis and limits of liability, the time limits for notice of claim, and the rules on lien and general average.

Where GML acts as a Multimodal Transport Operator, carriage is governed by the Multimodal Transportation of Goods Act, 1993 and the terms of the multimodal transport document issued. GML is registered with the Directorate General of Shipping, Government of India (MTO/DGS/369/MAR/2025) and holds a Federal Maritime Commission bond (number 91070) for United States traffic.

Carriers' liability for loss or damage is limited by the applicable convention or statute and is, in most cases, substantially less than the value of the goods. Cargo insurance is arranged only on written instruction. If you have not asked us to arrange it, it has not been arranged.
