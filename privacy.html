Privacy Policy

What we collect when you contact us, why we hold it, and how to have it removed.

On this page

  • 1. What we collect
  • 2. Why we hold it
  • 3. Who we share it with
  • 4. This website itself
  • 5. How long we keep it
  • 6. Your rights
  • 7. Security
  • 8. Contacting us

Greenwich Meridian Logistics (India) Pvt. Ltd. (“GML”) collects personal data in two situations: when you contact us through this website, and when we carry your cargo. This page covers both.

1. What we collect

When you use a form on this site. The forms here — quote request, schedule request, career application, tracking — do not transmit anything to us on their own. They compose a message in your own email client, which you then choose to send. What we receive is the email you sent: the details you filled in, your email address, and anything you attached.

When you become a customer. Names, job titles, business contact details, shipment and consignment details, commercial documents, customs and regulatory information, and the correspondence between us.

When you apply for a job. Your CV and the details you send with it.

We do not ask for, and do not want, special-category personal data. Please do not send it.

2. Why we hold it

  • To quote for, arrange and perform the transport of goods, which is the contract between us.
  • To meet legal obligations: customs and regulatory filings, tax records, dangerous goods declarations, sanctions and trade compliance screening.
  • To manage the relationship — billing, claims, and answering questions about shipments.
  • To consider you for employment where you have applied.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

3. Who we share it with

Shipping cargo requires sharing consignment information. Depending on the movement, that means: shipping lines and airlines; our group and associate offices in the UAE, Kuwait, Oman, Canada and the USA; network agents at origin or destination; CFS, terminal and warehouse operators; hauliers; customs brokers; customs and other government authorities; insurers and banks where the shipment involves them.

Because cargo crosses borders, this necessarily involves transferring information to the countries the cargo moves through. Where we appoint a service provider, we require them to use the data only for the purpose we gave it to them for.

4. This website itself

Cookies. This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising trackers.

Third-party requests. Two things load from outside: web fonts from Google Fonts on every page, and, on the currency converter page only, exchange rates from a public rate feed. Both requests come from your browser and expose your IP address to those services in the ordinary way. Nothing about you is sent to them by us.

Your browser. The clocks on the World Time page read your device's own time and time zone. That reading stays in your browser and is never transmitted.

Server logs. The server hosting this site may keep standard access logs, as web servers do.

5. How long we keep it

Shipment records are retained for as long as customs, tax and transport law require, and for as long as a claim could be brought. Enquiries that do not become business are kept only as long as they are useful. Unsuccessful job applications are held for a reasonable period in case a suitable role opens, unless you ask us to delete them.

6. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to confirm what personal data we hold about you, to correct it if it is wrong, to delete it, or to stop using it for a particular purpose. Write to info@gmlindia.net with enough detail to identify the records.

Some data we cannot delete on request: customs filings, transport documents and accounting records must be retained by law for fixed periods. Where that applies, we will tell you which records they are and why.

7. Security

Access to shipment and customer data is limited to staff who need it for their work. GML holds ISO 9001:2015 certification, is an Authorized Economic Operator with Indian Customs and participates in C-TPAT — programmes that carry documented handling and access requirements.

No transmission over the internet is completely secure. Please do not send commercially sensitive documents by unencrypted email if you would rather not; ask us and we will arrange another route.
