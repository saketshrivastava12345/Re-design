What each one buys you

Why these matter on your shipment

MTO registration with DG Shipping

A Multimodal Transport Operator registration is what allows GML to issue a through transport document covering more than one mode of carriage under the Multimodal Transportation of Goods Act. Without it, a door-to-door movement has to be sold as a chain of separate contracts.

FMC bond and OTI status

The Federal Maritime Commission licence and bond cover the carriage of cargo to and from the United States. The bond number is quoted above so a US-side counterparty can confirm the filing.

AEO and C-TPAT

Authorized Economic Operator status with Indian Customs and C-TPAT participation are both supply chain security programmes. In practice they mean fewer interventions, faster clearance and a documented chain of custody — which is exactly what matters on hazardous and high-value cargo.

IATA for Mumbai

IATA accreditation lets our air cargo desk deal directly with the carriers on rates, allocation and dangerous-goods acceptance rather than through a third party.

ISO 9001:2015

The quality management standard, held company-wide. It is the audit trail behind the quality policy: documented procedures, corrective action, and an external surveillance audit that keeps them honest.
