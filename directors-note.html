Director's Note

A word from our Directors

Air, surface and sea as modes of transportation, with the help of a global network and in-depth expertise in logistic technology.

“GML is at the forefront of delivering innovative domestic and international logistics solutions using air, surface and sea as modes of transportation with the help of a global network and in-depth expertise in logistic technology.”

Mr. Mihir KotechaDirector

“We build competent teams to leverage global carrier partners besides adding distinctive value to the tailored logistics needs with a single streamlined point of contact.”

Mr. Bhavesh ThakkerDirector

What the note means in practice

Three commitments

  1. Mode follows cargo

    Air, surface and sea are held in one house, so the recommendation is not shaped by what we happen to control. A shipment that belongs on a vessel is not quoted onto an aircraft to fill a contract.

  2. Competent teams, not handoffs

    The team that books your space is the team that clears the cargo and answers when the plan changes. Global carrier partners are leveraged behind one point of contact rather than in front of it.

  3. Technology in the background

    In-depth expertise in logistic technology means a globally uniform IT structure and fast handling of documents — the parts of a shipment that quietly decide whether it clears on time.

The business behind the note

Twenty years, twenty-one offices

Founded in 2002 and led by managers and owners who have been in the logistic industry for decades, GML India operates through 21 offices and employs more than 450+ members.
