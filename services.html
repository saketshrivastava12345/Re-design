How a shipment actually runs

Efficient, Reliable, and Cost-Effective Logistics Solutions

Air, surface and sea as modes of transportation, with the help of a global network and in-depth expertise in logistic technology.

  1. 01

    Road Haulage

    Ex-works pick up and door deliveries. Cargo is collected and moved to the consolidation point.

  2. 02

    CFS & Consolidation

    Container Freight Operations are performed at CFS, a warehouse where consolidation and deconsolidation of Cargo take place.

  3. 03

    Ocean Leg

    LCL, FCL and NVOCC services with master consolidating, fast handling of documents and a globally uniform IT structure.

  4. 04

    Global Network

    A vast network of partners and agents across the world, coordinated from a single streamlined point of contact.

  5. 05

    Air Leg

    Contracts with major carriers, third country shipments, custom clearance and hazardous cargo handling.
