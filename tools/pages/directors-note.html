What the note means in practice

Three commitments

  1. Mode follows cargo

    Air, surface and sea are held in one house, so the recommendation is not shaped by what we happen to control. A shipment that belongs on a vessel is not quoted onto an aircraft to fill a contract.

  2. Competent teams, not handoffs

    The team that books your space is the team that clears the cargo and answers when the plan changes. Global carrier partners are leveraged behind one point of contact rather than in front of it.

  3. Technology in the background

    In-depth expertise in logistic technology means a globally uniform IT structure and fast handling of documents — the parts of a shipment that quietly decide whether it clears on time.
