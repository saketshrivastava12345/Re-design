Who we are

Founded in 2002,
in Mumbai

Greenwich Meridian Logistics (India) Pvt. Ltd is one of the leading global logistics companies headquartered in Mumbai, India. Founded in 2002, GML is led by an experienced team of managers and owners who have been in the logistic industry for decades.

Our comprehensive suite of logistic solutions encompasses multimodal expertise and data-centric information advantage. We are competent to cater to diversified business needs including hazardous cargo.

Driven by a long-term vision, GML India operates through 21 offices and employs more than 450+ members. Air, surface and sea are all modes we work in, and the choice between them is made on what the cargo needs rather than on what we happen to control.

What that adds up to for a shipper is a single streamlined point of contact: one team that books the space, moves the box to it, clears it, and answers the phone when the plan changes.
