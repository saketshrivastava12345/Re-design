Warehousing

The pause between two legs

Warehousing and storage solutions across the GML group network, at the points where cargo has to wait: before a sailing, after a discharge, or between an import clearance and a delivery window.

Most storage requirements in freight are not really storage requirements. They are timing problems — a container that landed before the buyer's warehouse could take it, an export order that shipped in three tranches and has to leave in one, a tank that discharged before the next load was fixed.

Handled inside the same booking, that pause costs a storage line. Handled outside it, it costs demurrage, detention and a second haulage move.
