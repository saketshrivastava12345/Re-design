Which rate applies

Three different rates on one shipment

A single consignment can be converted at three different rates, on three different days, by three different parties. It is worth knowing which is which before reconciling an invoice.

  • The customs rate. For assessing duty, Indian Customs applies the exchange rate notified by CBIC, which is fixed for a period and does not move with the market. It is almost never the rate on your bank statement.
  • The bank rate. What you actually pay when the remittance is made, including the spread. This is the number that hits your accounts.
  • The reference rate. The mid-market rate you see here and on financial sites. Useful for estimating and for checking a quotation; not a rate anyone will transact at.

Freight invoices follow the booking. Where a quotation is given in a foreign currency, the conversion basis is stated on the quotation itself. If it is not clear on yours, ask before you accept it — not after the invoice.
