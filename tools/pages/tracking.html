Why there is no map on this page

Live shipment status lives in our operations system, alongside the carrier feeds, the customs status and the notes the desk made this morning. This website is not connected to it.

We could have put a moving dot on a map anyway. It would have looked better and told you less. Instead, this page takes your reference and hands it to the two routes that can actually answer: an email that reaches the operations desk with the reference already in the subject line, and a phone number answered by someone who can see the file.

When the tracking feed is connected, this same form will show the milestones directly — the reference you enter here will not change.

In a hurry? Call +91 22 6148 9999 and quote the reference. During Indian business hours that is normally the fastest route to a status.

Where to find your reference

Which number is which

HBL

House Bill of Lading

Issued by GML for an ocean shipment. It is the contract between you and us, and the number you will have been given at booking confirmation.

SB

Shipping Bill No.

The customs export declaration number, generated when the shipping bill is filed. Useful when you need the customs status rather than the carrier status.

HAWB

House Air Waybill

The air equivalent of the HBL, issued by GML for your consignment within a consolidation.

MAWB

Master Air Waybill

The airline's own number for the master consignment. An eleven-digit number beginning with the carrier's three-digit prefix.

No reference to hand?
