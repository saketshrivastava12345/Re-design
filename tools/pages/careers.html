Working here

People, first and last

We never lose sight of the fact that our existence and services are about people, both domestically and overseas.

Freight is a business of judgement under time pressure. A rate is easy to copy; knowing which carrier will actually accept a class 3 consolidation on a Thursday sailing is not. That knowledge lives in people, and it is built by giving them real files early.

GML India is led by managers and owners who have been in the logistic industry for decades, and most of our senior operations staff came up through the desks. If you want to learn this trade properly, the fastest way is to sit next to someone doing it.

Common questions

Before you apply

You do not list current openings. Why?

Because hiring in a 26-office network is continuous and local, and a list published on a website is out of date within weeks. Applications are read and held against the roles that open in the city you name. If you would rather ask first, write to info@gmlindia.net with the function and the city.

Do you take freshers?

Yes. Trainee intakes run in operations, documentation and customs. No prior freight experience is expected — an eye for detail and a tolerance for deadlines gets you further than a logistics certificate.

Which cities?

Any of the 26 on our locations page. Head office roles are in Powai, Mumbai; the branch network covers the gateway ports and the inland manufacturing clusters.

What happens after I apply?

Your application reaches the head office desk and is circulated to the branches hiring for that function. If there is a fit, someone from that office will contact you directly.

Questions before you apply?

Call the head office and ask for the HR desk.
