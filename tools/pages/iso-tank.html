ISO Tank

Import, export and the empty leg in between

GML provides end-to-end logistics solutions for ISO Tank transportation, from import to export, with dedicated Empty Yards at each of our import locations.

Tank logistics is not container logistics with a different box. A tank arrives full, is discharged, must be cleaned or held, and then has to find a load out — or it becomes an expensive empty. The import cycle for ISO Tanks is still under development in India, which is precisely why the empty yards matter.

Our objective is to develop the export cycle ex India, so that a tank landing at Nhava-Sheva has somewhere to go next rather than a repositioning cost attached to it.

Import

Three gateways, three empty yards

Import handling at Nhava-Sheva, Mundra and Chennai, with a dedicated empty yard at each location so a discharged tank has a home instead of a demurrage clock.

Haulage, discharge coordination, yard storage and the return leg are handled on one booking.

Common questions

Before you fix a tank

What do you need to quote a tank movement?

The product name and UN number, the quantity in litres or kilograms, loading and discharge points, the required tank type and pressure rating, and whether heating or a food-grade cleaning certificate is needed. For gases, the filling ratio and the valve configuration.

Do you handle the empty return?

Yes. Dedicated empty yards at Nhava-Sheva, Mundra and Chennai mean a discharged tank moves off the port clock and into storage while the next load is arranged, rather than accruing detention.

Is cleaning included?

Cleaning is arranged as part of the cycle and certified for the next product. The requirement depends entirely on the previous cargo, so it is priced against the actual last-three-loads history of the unit.

Can a tank carry hazardous product?

That is most of what tanks carry. Acceptance depends on the UN number, the packing group and the carrier's own list — see Hazardous Cargo for how the classes are handled.

Moving liquid or gas in bulk?

Send the product, the UN number and the lane. The tank desk will confirm equipment and acceptance.
