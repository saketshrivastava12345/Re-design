Warehousing

The pause between two legs

Warehousing and storage solutions across the GML group network, at the points where cargo has to wait: before a sailing, after a discharge, or between an import clearance and a delivery window.

Most storage requirements in freight are not really storage requirements. They are timing problems — a container that landed before the buyer's warehouse could take it, an export order that shipped in three tranches and has to leave in one, a tank that discharged before the next load was fixed.

Handled inside the same booking, that pause costs a storage line. Handled outside it, it costs demurrage, detention and a second haulage move.

What we hold, and where

Four kinds of storage

01

CFS handling

Container Freight Operations are performed at CFS, a warehouse where consolidation and deconsolidation of Cargo take place. Stuffing, de-stuffing, palletisation and customs examination support.

Ocean Cargo
02

Empty tank yards

Dedicated empty yards at each ISO tank import location — Nhava-Sheva, Mundra and Chennai — so a discharged tank comes off the port clock while the next load is arranged.

ISO Tank
03

Export consolidation

Cargo received from several suppliers over several days, checked, held and then shipped as one consignment against one bill.

04

Project staging

Holding and marshalling for project consignments where components arrive from different origins and must reach site in erection sequence.

Project Cargo

Group capability

Integrity Logistics

Dedicated warehousing across the group is operated by our associate Integrity Logistics, which runs the storage and distribution side of the network alongside GML's freight operations.

For a warehousing requirement in the UAE, the Abu Dhabi office is the direct route: sales@ilsuae.ae, +971 502103424. For everything in India, the head office desk arranges it on your existing booking.

Rates are quoted per requirement. Storage pricing turns on volume, dwell time, handling in and out, and whether the cargo is bonded — so it is quoted against your actual pattern rather than a rate card.
