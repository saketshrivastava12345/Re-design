Project Cargo

Every size, start to finish

As the global economy continues to gain momentum with cross-border logistics transportation at its forefront, GML takes pride in managing heavy international logistics of every size from start to finish in the interest of its global customers.

With unmatched dedication and commitment, GML is competent in delivering an array of global shipment projects from steel production to chemical plant, from the power plant to pharmaceutical, and beyond.

Our project cargo team also focuses on reducing the project complexity besides reducing the shipment cost — the two are usually the same problem. A route survey that finds the bridge early is cheaper than the crane that fixes it late.

How a project runs

From drawing to foundation

  1. Feasibility and route survey

    Dimensions and weights against the physical route: axle loads, bridge ratings, turning radii, overhead clearances and port lifting capacity. This is the stage where a project either gets simpler or gets expensive.

  2. Method and equipment

    Choice of breakbulk, flat rack, open top or ro-ro; the trailer configuration; whether a lift is a shore crane, a floating crane or the vessel's own gear. Lashing and stowage plans go with it.

  3. Permits and approvals

    Over-dimensional cargo permits, police escorts, utility clearances and port approvals. Each one has a lead time, and the lead times — not the transit — usually set the schedule.

  4. Execution and supervision

    Loading, sea fastening, the ocean leg, discharge and the final inland move to site, supervised end to end so that responsibility never sits in the gap between two contractors.
